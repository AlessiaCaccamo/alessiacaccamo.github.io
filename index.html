ABOUT

About me

I am a PhD researcher with a background in neuroscience and psychology. I hold a BSc and MRes in Neuroscience, am an Associate Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (AFHEA), and have experience in university teaching, assessment, and academic mentoring. My research has been published in scientific journals, and I’ve presented my work at international conferences and invited talks. Alongside research, I am passionate about scientific communication, teaching, and academic leadership. I’ve organised seminars and a wide range of events, chaired committees, and contributed to researcher skills training. I am a figure skater and have a strong interest in sport, performance, and the connection between the brain and human behaviour.

Neuroscience and Performance

Exploring the relationship between brain activity, mental states, cognition, and human performance through both research and practical applications. This work includes the measurement of brain activity using established and portable neurotechnologies, such as EEG, allowing neural responses to be investigated in naturalistic settings. I am particularly interested in translating neuroscience into practical approaches to brain health and wellbeing including neuroscience-informed brain health coaching.
